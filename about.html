Who We Are

Seamless events. Personal journeys.

Serene Moments by M&M is an event planning, coordination and tour operations company based in Rwanda. We specialise in creating seamless events and personalised travel experiences by combining thoughtful planning, professional coordination, reliable service providers and attention to detail.

From corporate conferences and exhibitions to weddings, private celebrations, retreats and tours, we work closely with our clients to understand their needs, manage the details and deliver experiences that are organised, meaningful and memorable.

Our approach is built on creativity, professionalism, flexibility and a commitment to making every client's experience as smooth and enjoyable as possible.
