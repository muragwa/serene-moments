Welcome to Serene Moments

Thoughtful planning, seamless experiences.

Serene Moments by M&M is an event planning, coordination and tour operations company based in Rwanda. We combine thoughtful planning, professional coordination, reliable service providers and attention to detail.

From corporate conferences and exhibitions to weddings, private celebrations, retreats and tours, we work closely with you to understand your needs, manage the details and deliver experiences that are organised, meaningful and memorable.

"You focus on what matters most — we take care of the details."

Our Approach

Clarity & peace of mind, at every stage.

Successful events and tours begin with understanding you. Here's how we work.

Step 01

Consultation

We discuss your objectives, preferences, budget and number of participants.

Step 02

Proposal

A tailored plan, suggested venues or destinations and a transparent quotation.

Step 03

Booking

We coordinate approved bookings, suppliers, transport, catering and equipment.

On-site Management

Step 05

Follow-up

We gather feedback and identify ways to make future experiences even better.
