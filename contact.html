Good to Know

Frequently asked questions

How is a quotation prepared?

Each quotation is developed according to the scope of work, number of participants, duration, selected venues, transport requirements and any additional services. Third-party expenses and applicable service fees are communicated to you before confirmation.

Can you handle only part of my event or trip?

Yes. We offer both end-to-end planning and tailored services — for example only venue sourcing, transport, accommodation or on-site coordination.

Which areas of Rwanda do you cover?

We're based in Kigali and source venues and destinations across Musanze, Rubavu, Karongi, Rusizi, Nyamasheke, Huye and other parts of Rwanda based on your needs.

Do you work with organisations and international visitors?

Absolutely. We serve companies, government institutions, NGOs, schools and universities, associations, tourists and international visitors, as well as individuals and families.

What happens after I send an enquiry?

We'll arrange a consultation to understand your objectives, preferences and budget, then prepare a tailored proposal. Once approved, we coordinate all bookings and manage everything on the day.
